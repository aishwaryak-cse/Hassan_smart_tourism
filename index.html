About Hassan Discover

A student-built concept for smarter local tourism.

The idea

Hassan Discover is designed to bring heritage destinations, nature spots, local experiences, trip planning and community knowledge into one friendly tourism portal.

The goal is simple: help visitors discover more while giving local people a way to surface places that deserve attention.

Contact / Feedback

Research note

Official tourism descriptions used in this prototype are sourced from the Hassan District, Government of Karnataka tourism portal. Current access rules, timings, tickets and conditions can change, so visitors should verify them before travelling.

For lesser-known locations supplied by the community, the portal avoids presenting unverified claims as facts.
